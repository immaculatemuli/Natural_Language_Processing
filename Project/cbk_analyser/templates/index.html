Key Numbers
At a glance

The four most important figures from this report — extracted automatically.

Central Bank Rate (CBR)
—
The rate CBK charges banks. When it rises, all bank loan rates follow — your loan repayments go up.
Inflation Rate
—
How fast prices are rising across Kenya. CBK's target range is 2.5% – 7.5%.
GDP Growth
—
How fast Kenya's economy is expanding. Above 5% is strong. Below 3% means fewer jobs and slower growth.
Forex Reserves
—
Kenya's foreign currency buffer. Keeps the shilling stable and allows imports of fuel, medicine, and equipment.
